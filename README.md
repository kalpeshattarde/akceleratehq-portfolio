## Projects & Business Impact

### 1. AI Lead Capture & Qualification

[Open case study](akceleratehq-lead-capture-automation/README.md)

**What I built:** A Google Sheets + n8n intake and routing system that normalizes enquiries, scores automation opportunities with structured AI output, updates lead status, sends confirmation, and routes leads into discovery, manual review, or nurture.

**How it saves time:** Replaces repetitive manual review of every enquiry with consistent scoring, field normalization, and automatic next-action routing.

**How it supports revenue:** High-intent enquiries can move into discovery scheduling faster, while lower-intent enquiries remain in a structured nurture path instead of consuming sales-team attention.

## Business Impact Framework

I design automation around three practical outcomes:

1. **Time saved** — remove repetitive entry, screening, routing, reminders, reporting, and coordination.
2. **Revenue capacity created** — move qualified leads faster, reduce missed follow-ups, support retention, and free people for higher-value work.
3. **Operational visibility** — turn scattered events into structured records, dashboards, alerts, logs, and next actions.

These are the intended business mechanisms of the systems. This public repository does not claim specific revenue increases, hours saved, or ROI unless those figures are explicitly documented and verified.

## How We Deliver

1. Understand the current business process.
2. Identify repetitive work, bottlenecks, delays, and failure points.
3. Design the workflow, data boundaries, approvals, and integrations.
4. Build the smallest useful version.
5. Test normal cases, edge cases, duplicates, failures, permissions, and provider limits.
6. Add logs, alerts, monitoring, documentation, and handover.
7. Improve the system using actual operating feedback.
