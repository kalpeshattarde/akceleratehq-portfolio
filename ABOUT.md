# About

## Kalpesh Attarde

**AI Automation Engineer | Business Process Automation Consultant**  
**AKcelerateHQ** · Mumbai, India

I design practical automation systems that connect business processes, APIs, communication channels, databases, and operational reporting. My focus is on the engineering around a workflow: understanding the process, defining rules, integrating existing tools, adding appropriate human review, and documenting what is required for reliable operation.

This repository is a portfolio of n8n workflow exports and architecture examples. It is not presented as a list of verified client deployments. Each project README identifies what the export demonstrates, what is described as a design direction, and what still requires testing, credentials, operational ownership, or production hardening.

## Working Principles

- Start with the business process and the people who operate it.
- Prefer deterministic validation and business rules for high-impact actions.
- Use AI for bounded classification, extraction, drafting, or decision support where appropriate.
- Keep sensitive, irreversible, financial, clinical, and public-facing actions under human control until they are validated.
- Treat logging, duplicate prevention, failure paths, access control, and handover as part of the delivery.
