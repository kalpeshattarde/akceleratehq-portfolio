# Delivery Proof

[Portfolio home](../README.md) · [Projects index](../PROJECTS.md) · [Implementation readiness](IMPLEMENTATION-READINESS.md)

This repository contains portfolio workflow exports, architecture examples, sample data, and delivery documentation.

## Evidence provided here

- Workflow definitions and technical identifiers
- Sanitized sample data and field maps
- Business-case, setup, security, and operations notes
- Architecture decisions and exception-handling guidance
- Offline simulation or reviewer walkthroughs where available

## Claims this evidence does not establish

- Live external execution or customer adoption
- Business performance or financial outcomes
- Regulatory compliance or production approval
- Long-term reliability under production load
- Guaranteed delivery or operational performance

Before production use, validate credentials, permissions, schemas, normal and failure paths, monitoring, support ownership, and acceptance criteria with the responsible stakeholders.
