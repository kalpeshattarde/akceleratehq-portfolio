# Client Discovery Checklist

[Portfolio home](../README.md) · [Implementation readiness](IMPLEMENTATION-READINESS.md)

Use this checklist to establish the business, process, data, and ownership boundaries before implementation.

## Business and process

- [ ] Business owner and decision-maker identified
- [ ] Current process, users, handoffs, and pain points mapped
- [ ] Desired outcome and measurable success criteria defined
- [ ] Trigger events, approvals, exceptions, and escalation paths documented
- [ ] Manual steps that must remain human-controlled identified

## Systems and data

- [ ] Systems, integrations, and data owners listed
- [ ] Input and output fields documented against real schemas
- [ ] Missing, duplicate, malformed, and sensitive data scenarios reviewed
- [ ] Authentication, permissions, rate limits, and vendor constraints checked
- [ ] Retention, privacy, and compliance requirements confirmed

## Operations and delivery

- [ ] Error handling, alerts, rollback, and support ownership defined
- [ ] Representative success and failure cases available for testing
- [ ] Acceptance criteria agreed with operators
- [ ] Known limitations and exclusions documented
- [ ] Production activation treated as a separate approval decision

A workflow export or prototype is not evidence of live deployment, business results, or compliance.
